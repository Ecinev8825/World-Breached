Prologue - A Silencing Song

??? - March 29, 2033

A quiet song echoed throughout the hollow. Calming but mysterious. It was unusual since no one lived or went in this part of the forest. Despite all the ominous signs, he still kept following that hauntingly beautiful voice. It was like the voice was possessing him.

Once he got to the middle of the hollow, the voice abruptly stopped, leaving the area in complete silence.

“Uhm, hello?” he asked, slightly confused. “Why did you stop singing?” He gazed around anxiously as a dark shadow started looming over him.

“Who are you and why-” then suddenly, silver flashed across his face as he felt a piercing pain in his neck. It all happened so fast. One moment, he was following that melodic voice; the next, he was gasping for air while his blood poured out of his neck. He clutched his neck, blood pooling into his hands

“Oh dear child, what a shame -for you, at least.” The figure snickered as he fell to the ground. It hurt so, so much, and oh, how much he wanted this torture to end. And as it all faded to black, e heard the person speak once more.

“You will be perfect.”

Broken Wishes

Chapter 3 - A Free Stay

Zephyr - July 29th, 2046

Zephyr was the first one all the way through and also the first to be able to take in the view. This was literally a dream come true for her, since it was the complete opposite of her home world.

There was sunlight, clear skies, lush foliage, did she mention sunlight? The field stretched as far as she could see, dotted with patches of white and blue flowers. Clouds hung low in the sky and the sun shone through the gaps, filling the area with a warm glow.

She slightly turned around to her team, gesturing at the scene wildly in excitement, even signing so quickly about it that maybe even Xylo didn’t catch it. But then, she noticed that they seemed distracted. Assuming they were making a plan, she followed along by pressing a button on her Multi-Band, kinda uncreative, not gonna lie, to see which direction they would be going in. The metallic arrows whirled around her wrist before settling on the starting point of their route.

And, it pointed in the opposite direction.

She was very a bit disappointed since that would mean no grassy field roaming. Zephyr reluctantly turned around to see a really dense forest. Roots covered the forest floor, moss and various plants climbed almost everything, and small rays of sun peeked through the thick leafy branches. Well, this is going to be a pain to get through.

“Zeph, why don’t you do some scouting in the trees since you’re the best climber?” Xylo said, putting a paw on her shoulder.

She nodded and started making her way up the nearest trunk while the others walked beneath her. She jumped between branches, occasionally pausing to survey the area in front of them. Wait... She spotted a dirt path near where they were walking. That could lead to the town! She needed to tell someone.

Zephyr observed that Eclipse happened to be the closest person to her. She grabbed a pinecone from the nearest tree limb and threw it at him.

“ACK- QUÉ?!?!”

“Dirt path to the left.”

Eclipse looked at her with annoyance, a plan for revenge burning in his eyes. He then made a slitting motion at his throat and made a face at her, still glaring. She might be due for some elaborate prank at the dorms when the mission was done, but that was for future her to worry about.

Eclipse stopped making murder-eyes at her and told the team what she saw. They all turned toward that path in the distance to see where it led.

—

“Guys,” Kieres said, “where are we?”

They had been walking on that path for at least an hour. She decided around 30 minutes ago that she was tired of the trees and hopped down to join her team. Suddenly, she spotted something- or a few somethings in the distance. Turns out, it was three of those tall, deer-looking animals that Director Sallow showed them in the meeting earlier today. Can’t hurt to befriend one, right?

Zephyr walked towards the creatures, slowly, not to scare them. They looked even cooler up close; clean white coats with light blue accents. They stared at her, zero emotions showing through their eyes. Then, something weird happened.

One of the deer-thing’s faces started splitting apart.

First, a small crack at the tip of its muzzle, then branching all the way to the bottom of its jaw and the top of its head. Its head fully opened to show its rows upon rows of sharp teeth.

She stood very still, then really slowly started backing up. The creature looked at her with what was probably curiosity and contemplation.

Suddenly, it lunged at her.

Zephyr scrambled back just in time to barely avoid the deer-thing’s hoofs. She started running back to her group, the animal still on her tail. After fumbling around to grab the whistle on the cord on her neck, she gripped it and blew into the mouthpiece as hard as she could. The piece of metal let out an ear-piercing screech, and everyone turned to the noise.

All of them looked at her, then at the creature behind her, in shock. Briar started pointing at each one of her members, shouting orders, while Zephyr jumped up on a tree to save her tail. What she didn’t plan was that the deer-thing would switch targets that quickly.

The others unsheathed their weapons while Xylo climbed up next to her a few seconds later.

“Zeph, what did you do to get them this agitated?” he asked, loading one of his mini laser cartridges in his gun.

“I don’t know, they just opened their weird mouths at me!” she signed. Zephyr hooked her claws around one of what she liked to call smokey-daggers and hopped off the branch just as the others on the ground were starting to get surrounded by the other two deer-things.

She threw the dagger near one of the animal’s hooves, trying not to hurt them, which created a loud bang and a huge cloud of dust due to the dirt. Through the dust cloud, she saw the others move around the creatures so they weren’t cornered anymore. They slowly started backing out of the haze, appearing somewhere behind her.

Now that they couldn’t see her, she could maybe elbow one of them in the head, or sweep their legs so they fall over. She reached down to grab another dagger from her belt. She took a step closer, trying to pinpoint the direction she should be throwing in.

Nothing seemed to be moving though. Her team, probably deciding that there was no use waiting for those animals to come back, turned around and walked away. She turned around and started to leave with them when something huge suddenly pushed her down to the mossy ground. A hoof dug into Zephyr’s spine, keeping her pinned to the forest floor.

She glanced up at the menacing jaws full of teeth, dagger still clutched in her claws.

“ZEPH!!” Xylo cried. He slid off the branch he was sitting on and frantically started making his way towards her.

Nearing her, the deer-creature took notice and stood between them, making some kind of growling noise. Saisei, close behind Xylo, was hissing and arching her back at them, tail still filled with crackling electricity.

Briar, Eclipse, and Kieres soon made a wide semi-circle around the whole mess, weapons ready to strike.


“Wisteria, Oleander, Hickory, back down. They are harmless.”


A person with three pairs of feathery wings (Wow, that’s a lotta wings) wearing a kimono of sorts and a blindfold landed behind them. The deer-thing standing on her stepped off, and all three of them went over to greet their owner.

Xylo pulled her up to her feet. All of the others slightly lowered their weapons, but were still wary enough not to fully put them away.

“I see my guards have unintentionally assumed you all are threats and attacked.” they said, walking closer.

Some of them backed up instinctively. The person noticed, stopped moving and tried verbal reasoning.

“I assure you, I mean no harm. I can give you a place to stay if you would like. I know not many people come out this far without a reason.” the stranger explained. They took a few steps closer while their ‘guards’ stood near where he landed earlier.

Zephyr spotted two weird sparkle-shaped things circling the unknown person she hadn’t seen before. Looking closer, she realized the sparkles had eyes on them. She assumed they had these things floating around them because of the blindfold they were wearing, which was weird, cuz why do you need a blindfold?

“Okay,” Briar said, gathering their team in a circle of sorts, “it’s either we go follow some stranger or camp out here with who knows what.”

Zephyr stole another glance at the really tall deer things. If they were this strong and scary on their own, she wasn’t going to stay in this forest to see what else lurked in these shadows at night. She gazed back at her friends, and it seemed like they all felt the same.

“Hold on... Hey wing guy! Where are you gonna take us anyway?” Eclipse asked, turning to the mystery person.

“Everdawn Crest.”

“Isn’t that where we’re going?” Eclipse said, almost matter-of-factly. “It would be a lot easier to have a free hotel or something. Plus, we could get some info out of our feathery friend.”

Briar’s expression was neutral, but her eyes glinted with suspicion. “You guys okay with going there?” She watched them all nod in agreement. “Fine, we’ll go with you.” she said.

“Splendid! You all are going to love the town.” they exclaimed. They walked through their loose circle.

“Come along everyone, let’s go!”
