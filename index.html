A Silencing Song

Prologue - A Silencing Song

??? - March 29, 2033

A quiet song echoed throughout the hollow. Calming but mysterious. It was unusual since no one lived or went in this part of the forest. Despite all the ominous signs, he still kept following that hauntingly beautiful voice. It was like the voice was possessing him.

Once he got to the middle of the hollow, the voice abruptly stopped, leaving the area in complete silence.

“Uhm, hello?” he asked, slightly confused. “Why did you stop singing?” He gazed around anxiously as a dark shadow started looming over him.

“Who are you and why-” then suddenly, silver flashed across his face as he felt a piercing pain in his neck. It all happened so fast. One moment, he was following that melodic voice; the next, he was gasping for air while his blood poured out of his neck. He clutched his neck, blood pooling into his hands

“Oh dear child, what a shame -for you, at least.” The figure snickered as he fell to the ground. It hurt so, so much, and oh, how much he wanted this torture to end. And as it all faded to black, e heard the person speak once more.

“You will be perfect.”

Chapter 1 - Finishing a Session

Eclipse - July 29th, 2046

Ever wanted to be torn apart by a giant wyvern’s huge claws and get turned into a shredded fox pancake?

Turns out that was going to be Eclipse soon because those huge claws were diving right towards him.

“UHH, GUYS, A LITTLE HELP HERE?!”

This was totally the end.

*BANG*

“Oh, thanks, Xylo!”

“You're welcome!” Xylo responded.

Good thing his electronic bestie came in with their laser gun to save him from his untimely demise. Though there were still more to deal with. At least a dozen dragon hunters, along with their five dragons to be exact.

“Hey, pretty boy! Get over here! I need backup.” Briar called over her shoulder. Eclipse turned around to see that she was starting to be surrounded by five of the hunters.

“Fineee, I’ll help since you need it so much.” he teased as he cleanly decapitated one of them with his scythe.

Briar also fought, whipping her sword at them, which caused some of them to disintegrate due to the gashes along their bodies. “I hate you.”

“¡Yo también te amo, hermana! (Love you too, sis!).” He called back, happily.

Despite being outnumbered, they easily took out all the hunters.

The next foe they were up against was another dragon hunter on top of a drake. Despite it being out for their throats, he couldn’t help but admire it.

Shiny teal scales, pinkish-red accents, and cream colored underscales to pull it all together. What’s not to love? Eclipse thought. He did have to move to the left, so he didn’t get trampled.

Briar raised her hand to the other side of the drake, summoning thick vines to restrict its movement. Through its thrashing, he managed to climb on top and kill the hunter. Eclipse pulled his scythe over his shoulder, preparing to slice the drake’s neck from the back. Under his feet, though, he felt its scales heat up and saw a fiery low between them.

“Briar, watch out!” He shouted, jumping off its neck and tackling her out of the way. As he assumed, the drake’s mouth started spewing a stream of fire, disintegrating Briar’s vines.

“So, got any plans?”

“Do you wanna go up in person with that sword of yours?”

“Do I have a choice...?”

“Nope.”

Raising her hand once more, Briar summoned back her vines to strangle its claws. He used his telekinesis to give the vines as much extra binding as he could.

“Now, Eclipse!”

He held up his scythe, which shifted into its sword form. Eclipse aimed for that reptile’s throat while Briar summoned a few more tendrils to pull its head upwards. The scales glowed again as another stream of fire came out of its mouth, making the drake vaguely resemble a volcano.

With a quick slash at its scaly hide, the drake let out a final roar and collapsed, disintegrating into the same pixels the last enemies did.

Suddenly, a shrill whistle cut through the sounds of exploding gunfire and the slashing of weapons.

It was Zephyr, and she needed help because she was about to get absolutely destroyed by the last dragon.

Briar and Eclipse rushed over with Xylo following close behind to help as well. This pitch-black lizard was huge compared to the others, standing 15 feet tall and having a 50-foot wingspan. Its piercing gold gaze looked at them hungrily, probably choosing which one of them to eat up first.

Fortunately, his cat, Saisei, was flying just above it. With a very well-shot laser, Xylo was able to give her enough electricity for her to deflect into the dragon’s eye.

It roared in agony while the others charged at it. They were all attacking at full force, slashing, stabbing, shooting, anything to take it down.

This dragon, in particular, would be tough to kill, since you would have to do a lot of damage to it. This balanced out its fatal weakness: fire.

They couldn’t deal that much damage before getting swatted away by the dragon’s tail. The onyx dragon went in for the kill while they braced for the impact of those horrible claws and teeth.

The thing is, it surprisingly never came.

When they opened their eyes, they were met with the sight of Kieres standing over the dragon’s disintegrating body. Flaming guandao still in hand.

“You’re welcome.”

*beep beep beep* Session Completed

“Phew! We finally beat the level, guys!” Xylo said excitedly.

Kieres started helping them up after their “tragic” falls while he held his weapon in his other hand, waiting for the flame to extinguish. “Why do I always have to save you guys?” Kieres asked, holding his hand out to him.

“Well,” Eclipse said, grabbing his hand. “That sounds like a 'you' problem, Kier.” Kieres elbowed him in return.

Both of them were still jokingly arguing when the system’s voice started speaking again.

“Congratulations, Group 6! You have completed Level 4 successfully! Here are your scores:”

A screen above them lit up, showing a list of numbers. The numbers were randomized, changing every few seconds.

They all gathered in front of it, waiting in anticipation.

“Zephyr Ellis Silverwood; Female, Age: 20, and Saisei; Female, Age: 7, tied at 5th place with a score of 151.” The echoing voice said.

The screen zoomed in on the last two places, revealing their names along with their scores.

“I guess it wasn’t our day today.” Zephyr signed. She knelt down and offered Saisei a high-five. The feline meowed in response, returning the gesture with her paw.

Eclipse smiled while watching them interact. He was happy to see that Saisei was getting closer to their other team members, especially after what happened in her past.
